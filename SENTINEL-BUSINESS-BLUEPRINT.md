# Sentinel Business Blueprint

## Positioning
Financial control infrastructure for autonomous AI agents.

**Core promise:** AI agents can act autonomously. Sentinel controls their money.

## Beachhead
AI-native companies deploying autonomous agents across research, procurement, cloud operations, data acquisition and other workflows.

## Problem
Agents increasingly need to spend money on APIs, datasets, compute, software and services. Companies need financial authority controls: how much can each agent spend, where can it pay, when is human approval required, how are anomalous payments stopped, and how is every decision audited?

## Product
- Organization and treasury control
- Agent financial identities
- Policy engine
- Dynamic financial firewall
- Payment intents
- Human approvals
- Fleet-wide kill switch
- Transaction/audit history
- Explainable decision traces
- Risk and spend intelligence
- Automation and treasury replenishment

## Tempo role
Tempo is the first and deepest execution layer. Sentinel uses the Tempo payment rail for settlement while keeping organizational authority in the Sentinel policy layer. Future integrations can leverage Tempo access keys, spending limits, fee sponsorship, memos, batching, scheduled/concurrent transactions and machine-payment flows where appropriate.

## Differentiation
Sentinel is not another wallet or generic AI-payment API. The wedge is **fleet-level financial governance**:

**Organization → Treasury → Agent Fleet → Policies → Payments → Audit → Intelligence**

The product answers not only “can this payment execute?” but “is this agent authorized to spend this money, with this recipient, under these conditions, right now?”

## Business model hypothesis
Free developer tier for experimentation, paid team/business subscriptions, and custom enterprise contracts. Usage can expand with fleet size and financial activity. Pricing must be validated with customers.

## Go-to-market
1. Developer-first SDK/API and demo environment.
2. Integrate with agent platforms and AI-native startups.
3. Expand into enterprise governance, finance and security teams.

## Product principle
Every feature must strengthen financial authority, agent autonomy, organizational control, or trust/auditability.
